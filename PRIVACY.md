# Privacy Policy for AI Text Rewriter

**Last updated: June 2, 2026**

## Data Collection

AI Text Rewriter does **not** collect, store, or log any personal information, browsing history, or user data.

## What Data Is Transmitted

The only data transmitted by this extension is the text you explicitly select for rewriting. This text is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider API you configure (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, or your custom endpoint). The extension acts solely as a conduit between your browser and your chosen AI provider.

## Data Storage

- Your API key and settings are stored **locally** in Chrome's secure storage (`chrome.storage.local`) and never leave your device.
- No text you select or rewrite is stored by the extension.
- No analytics, telemetry, or tracking mechanisms are included.

## Third-Party Services

The extension communicates with the AI provider API you choose to configure. Please refer to the privacy policy of your chosen provider:

- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
- Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- DeepSeek: https://deepseek.com/privacy

## Security

API keys are stored in Chrome's local storage, which is sandboxed per extension. The extension uses HTTPS for all API communications.
